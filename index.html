Foxjam Collective

Many hands, many rhythms, one jam. We mix sounds from around the world into music that moves between traditions instead of staying inside one.

Music without borders

Foxjam is a collective of musicians who play all kinds of music. Some of us come from folk, some from jazz, some from electronic music. We meet in the middle and see what happens.

Every track starts as a jam. Rhythms from one part of the world meet melodies from another, and the song grows from there.

What you might hear

  • Afrobeat grooves
  • Balkan brass
  • Latin percussion
  • Nordic folk
  • Desert blues
  • Dub & reggae
  • Odd meters
  • Electronic textures
  • Improvisation
